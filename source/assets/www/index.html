No Axon signals right now. The scanner listens for Axon body cameras, Tasers, and Fleet units by their public Bluetooth signatures.

How it works: Axon gear broadcasts Bluetooth signals that identify the maker. This app listens passively — it never connects to, pairs with, or transmits to any device.

Range is rough: signal strength gives a ballpark band, not a distance. Walls, vehicles, and pockets weaken signals a lot. Treat “near” as “somewhere around you,” never an exact spot.

Privacy: signals are matched in memory on your phone only. No device IDs are stored, logged, or sent anywhere. The list clears itself seconds after a signal is gone.

Limits: scanning runs while the app is open. Android pauses some matching when the screen sleeps. Not every officer or patrol car carries detectable gear, and an Axon hit can also be a holster or vehicle unit.
